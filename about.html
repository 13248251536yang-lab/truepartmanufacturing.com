WHO WE ARE

About
Truepart.

At Truepart, we make custom manufacturing easier and more accessible. We help businesses worldwide turn their designs and ideas into high-quality precision components through reliable manufacturing solutions.

We specialize in CNC machining and precision metal components, supported by our own core manufacturing base and a trusted network of manufacturing partners across China. This combination allows us to offer flexible manufacturing capabilities and dependable production solutions.

From rapid prototyping to small-batch and volume production, we work closely with our customers to meet their project requirements. Our team coordinates every stage of the process, from quotation and engineering communication to production and delivery.

At Truepart, we are committed to making manufacturing simple, reliable, and efficient.
