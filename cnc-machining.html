Process Overview

Find the Right Machining Process

Different machining technologies offer different advantages. We help identify a suitable process based on your part design and project requirements.

3-Axis CNC Milling

3-axis milling moves the cutting tool along the X, Y and Z axes. It is commonly used for flat surfaces, pockets, slots, holes and many standard machined components.

4-Axis CNC Milling

4-axis milling adds a rotary axis to conventional 3-axis machining. This allows parts to be positioned and machined from additional angles, reducing repositioning operations for suitable designs.

5-Axis CNC Machining

5-axis machining combines three linear axes with two rotary axes. It is suitable for complex surfaces, angled features and components that require machining from multiple directions.

CNC Turning

CNC turning rotates the workpiece while a cutting tool removes material. It is widely used for cylindrical parts such as shafts, pins, bushings and threaded components.

Mill-Turn Machining

Mill-turn machining combines turning and milling operations in one machine platform. It can help manufacture complex components with multiple features while reducing the need for separate machine setups.

Swiss-Type CNC Machining

Swiss-type machining is particularly suitable for small, slender and precision components. The sliding headstock design provides support close to the cutting area during machining.
